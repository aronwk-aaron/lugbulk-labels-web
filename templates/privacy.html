Privacy policy

LUGBulk Labels · last updated September 30, 2026

LUGBulk Labels makes printable labels and packing reports from a LEGO User Group's LUGBulk order sheet. This page explains what it does with your information.

Uploaded spreadsheets

A file you upload is read in memory to make the files you asked for, then discarded. It is not stored, and the labels and reports made from it are only sent back to you.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, the app asks Google for:

  • Your email address, to know who you are and show it on the page.
  • Access to the Google Drive files you choose with this app (drive.file). The app can only open spreadsheets you pick with its “Pick a Google Sheet” button, and only reads their order data. It cannot see, change or delete anything else in your Drive.

The app stores: your Google account id and email; a Google sign-in token, encrypted, so it can read your picked sheets when you ask; the sheets you saved (their Google file id and name); the label design for each sheet; and a short history of downloads (report type, time, count). Your sheet's contents are read when you check, preview or download, and are not kept (a preview may reuse a copy held in memory for up to two minutes).

Google user data

LUGBulk Labels' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to make the labels and reports you request. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. The page may remember your theme and upload label design in your browser's local storage. There are no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Remove a saved sheet from your list at any time. To delete your account and everything stored about you, email admin@aronwk.com. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google Account settings.
